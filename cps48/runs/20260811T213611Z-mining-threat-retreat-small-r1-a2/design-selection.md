# Approved responsibility-allocation decision

## Decision basis and evidence separation

DS-BASIS-01: The frozen seed requires two implementation-independent allocations to be compared while preserving one principal decision owner, the eleven-case finite partition, thirteen semantic domain interactions, and the `small` campaign bounds. The Designer resolution may clarify testability and trace syntax but may not change domain priority, outputs, recovery, or exclusions.

DS-BASIS-02: Handbook-derived guidance supplies the obligation to compare alternatives with explicit criteria and retained rationale. It does not favor either mining-domain allocation or establish tool realizability.

DS-BASIS-03: The finite values, priorities, admissibility rules, and recovery sequence are frozen pilot abstractions. Qualification and command-delivery responsibility may be distributed, but current phase, priority, all five outputs, and all eleven cases remain with one principal owner.

## Alternatives considered

ALT-01: Use distinct qualification duties for threat, resources, and request/availability around the principal owner, plus separate mining, defense, and movement/service execution duties. This allocation has seven responsibility-bearing semantic usages and thirteen end-to-end semantic interactions.

ALT-02: Use one consolidated input-qualification duty around the same principal owner while retaining the three separate execution duties. This allocation has five responsibility-bearing semantic usages and the same thirteen end-to-end semantic interactions.

## Selection criteria and comparison

DS-CRIT-01: Both alternatives preserve the exact eight monitored and five controlled end-to-end domain interactions without inventing messages to satisfy a metric.

DS-CRIT-02: Both alternatives preserve exactly one owner of current and next phase, priority, case selection, and all five controlled decisions.

DS-CRIT-03: ALT-01 better localizes exact value/status preservation by input family and makes threat, resource, and request/availability defects independently inspectable without distributing disposition policy.

DS-CRIT-04: ALT-01 better separates the three command-delivery domains while preserving each selected command and without inferring command acceptance or physical success.

DS-CRIT-05: ALT-01 keeps retreat and service progression with the principal owner and leaves the movement/service duty responsible only for unchanged command delivery, so recovery ordering is not duplicated.

DS-CRIT-06: ALT-02 is structurally smaller, but its consolidated qualifier couples input families with distinct validity and policy concerns and provides less localized contract evidence. It provides no behavioral or interaction-count advantage.

## Final selection and exact metrics

DS-DEC-01: ALT-01 is approved. The final semantic architecture is exactly seven responsibility-bearing usages: one principal owner, three qualification duties, and three execution duties. The final abstract also has exactly thirteen typed directed end-to-end semantic interactions, eleven nontrivial decision cases, five scenario families, one explicit root system usage, containment depth exactly `2` for all seven semantic usages beneath the root, and one weakly connected semantic region.

DS-DEC-02: The exact `7/13/11/5` point remains within the `small` bounds of `5..8` semantic usages, `7..14` semantic interactions, `6..12` nontrivial contract cases, and `4..6` scenario families. None of these counts includes padding, context actors, the root, generated-support routing, or local pass-through identity obligations.

DS-RATIONALE-01: ALT-01 best satisfies responsibility cohesion, local-contract expressibility, end-to-end reviewability, and fault localization while leaving all disposition authority and state in one place. The Model review identified no reason to change this allocation.

## Realization and generated-support allowance

DS-REALIZE-01: Generation is authorized to realize one root System containing one behavior-free support Process and seven semantic Thread usages corresponding exactly to the seven selected responsibilities. This is an approved concrete mapping of ALT-01, not an eighth semantic responsibility or a reusable mining-domain template.

DS-REALIZE-02: Routing-only ports, forwarding connections, structured value/status wrappers, and the behavior-free support Process may be marked `@generated-support` only with a specific trace or rationale. Routing hops refine INT-01 through INT-13 and are not additional semantic interactions.

DS-REALIZE-03: Generated support may not own current phase, priority, case selection, output decisions, transformation, filtering, domain policy, command override, or inferred physical success. Any helper with such responsibility would require a new Designer baseline and is not authorized by this decision.

## Local-contract expectations

DS-CONTRACT-01: The three qualification duties shall preserve exact input values and their independent statuses: threat as one pair; cargo and capacitor as two pairs; and operator request plus three availabilities as four pairs. They shall not collapse the eight statuses or choose a disposition.

DS-CONTRACT-02: The three execution duties shall preserve the principal owner's selected mining, defense, and movement/service commands exactly through delivery. They shall not infer acceptance, completion, neutralization, or other physical results.

DS-CONTRACT-03: The principal owner alone shall initialize and update phase state and express all eleven complete five-output cases. Every priority exclusion shall be explicit; textual order or unsupported first-match semantics is prohibited.

DS-CONTRACT-04: Qualifier and executor identity guarantees are local non-case obligations and do not increase the eleven-case count. Pure forwarding elements may have identity contracts, but such contracts are optional and may not add domain policy.

## Bounded end-to-end analysis obligations

DS-ANALYSIS-01: The retained analyzer shall begin from the independent raw product of 4,976,640 snapshots and use the approved fourteen-pair phase/progress relation, independently of validity, to enumerate exactly 2,322,432 admissible snapshots under the 180-second harness watchdog.

DS-ANALYSIS-02: The analyzer shall compose the three qualifier relations, the principal owner's stateful relation, the three command-preservation relations, and the explicit thirteen-interaction map. It shall check exactly one case, all five assigned outputs, exact input-fault equivalence, local identity, resolved endpoints, and retained witnesses for any failure.

DS-ANALYSIS-03: Separate retained traces shall cover all five scenario families and the complete recovery sequence, including validity restoration, retreat-availability loss and restoration, and the fresh-request rule. These results are bounded conformance evidence, not compositional proof, solver certification, or physical validation.

## Model-review resolution and consequences

DS-REV-01: The paired contract declarations are revised to the parser-supported `ID / ID:` form. This is a traceability repair only; all eleven identifiers, guards, results, priorities, and counts are unchanged.

DS-REV-02: The admissible current-phase/service-progress predicate is revised to the exact independent relation requested by the Model: `Away` with `Idle`, `Mining`, `Defending`, `Retreating`, or `Fault_Hold`; `At_Station` with `Retreating` or `Fault_Hold`; `Docked_Loaded` with `Retreating`, `Servicing`, or `Fault_Hold`; and either confirmation value with `Servicing` or `Fault_Hold`. This removes analysis ambiguity without deriving admissibility from the case guards.

DS-CONSEQ-01: Model regeneration is authorized against `system-abstract.md` and this approved selection. Generation shall not weaken the frozen policy, add a second decision owner, reinterpret a routing hop as a semantic interaction, or claim that bounded checks prove the domain behavior.

DS-DEFER-02: Compositional or formal proof, solver certification, unbounded correctness, physical docking/offload/resupply, hostile neutralization, later redeployment, mission success, profitability, survivability, safety, certification, and production readiness remain `deferred` or `out-of-scope`.
