# Mining cargo/docking domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 Small, Medium, and Hard systems all center station
cargo-transfer verification. They admit an offload attempt, reconcile ship and
warehouse evidence, govern cancellation/service faults and recovery, and
publish transfer or verification outcomes.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another cargo-transfer verifier, offload-
evidence coordinator, ship/warehouse reconciliation protocol, or renamed or
expanded version of that transaction. Select a different primary controlled
purpose grounded by the seed: ore-hold supervision before offload, docking and
undocking service-access supervision, or capacitor/support-system resupply
verification. Cargo transfer may be external context but must not remain the
organizing state machine or completion claim.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
closest replicate-1 baseline and identify at least two genuine differences
among boundary, state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and
recovery policy.
