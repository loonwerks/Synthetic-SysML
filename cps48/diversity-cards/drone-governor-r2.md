# Drone logical domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 Small, Medium, and Hard systems all make lifecycle
transition governance their primary controlled purpose: they consume requests
and observations, own or derive `Off/Standby/Charging/Ready/Flying`, arbitrate
priority and recovery, and publish lifecycle/enablement outcomes.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another lifecycle governor, mission governor,
or a renamed/elaborated version of that state machine. Select a different
primary controlled purpose grounded by the seed, preferably battery-awareness
and charging qualification or flight-readiness evidence coordination. Lifecycle
may be contextual input or advisory output, but it must not be the principal
controlled state or the organizing contract partition.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
lifecycle baseline and identify at least two genuine differences among boundary,
state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and recovery policy.
