# Vehicle domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 systems use temperature/health supervision, start
readiness, or protected start and power enablement as their primary controlled
purpose. Across the three tiers they qualify temperature, maintenance,
self-test, brake, and power evidence; own start or health state; and publish
authorization, power, mode, or status outcomes.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another temperature/health supervisor, start-
readiness gate, protected-start controller, power-enablement supervisor, or a
renamed/elaborated version of those policies. Select a different primary
controlled purpose grounded by the seed, preferably controller operating-mode
coordination around source-named start/on/off/maintenance/degraded indications,
or parking-brake supervisory coordination with start and power only as external
context. Do not reconstruct replicate-1 readiness or thermal-clearance policy.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
closest replicate-1 baseline and identify at least two genuine differences
among boundary, state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and
recovery policy.
