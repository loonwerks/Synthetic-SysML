# Forest-observation drone domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 Small and Hard systems control observation-sortie
eligibility, while Medium controls observation acquisition and radio delivery.
Together they cover mission eligibility, energy/navigation/sensing evidence,
observation availability, and delivery-oriented recovery.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another sortie-eligibility governor,
observation/data-delivery coordinator, or renamed/elaborated composition of
those systems. Select a different primary controlled purpose grounded by the
seed: battery-status assessment for drone-control use, navigation and obstacle-
awareness supervision, or power-and-propulsion availability. Observation and
sortie state may be external context but must not organize the contract
partition or principal controlled outcome.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
closest replicate-1 baseline and identify at least two genuine differences
among boundary, state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and
recovery policy.
