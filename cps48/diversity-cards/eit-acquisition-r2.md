# EIT domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 systems all center monitoring start and impedance-data
acquisition. They qualify preparation or authorization, govern an acquisition
session, and publish monitoring/acquisition outcomes; the hard system scales the
same controlled purpose with more state, evidence, fault, and recovery owners.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another monitoring-start gate, impedance-
acquisition coordinator, acquisition-session supervisor, or renamed/elaborated
version of that pipeline. Select a different primary controlled purpose grounded
by the seed: electrode-belt preparation and calibration support, or EIT-image
generation/analysis availability with acquisition treated only as an external
upstream event. Do not rebuild acquisition authorization as the organizing
state machine.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
closest replicate-1 baseline and identify at least two genuine differences
among boundary, state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and
recovery policy.
