# Mining-frigate domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 Small, Medium, and Hard systems are all combined
mining-threat-retreat supervisors. They centrally or compositionally arbitrate
among mining, defense, retreat, hold, and service using threat, cargo,
capacitor, availability, and recovery state, then publish a mission phase or
mode and selected action/reason.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another combined mission/action governor,
mining-versus-defense/retreat arbiter, or renamed/repartitioned version of that
policy. Select one narrower source-grounded primary controlled purpose:

- threat evidence, reporting, defense activation, and retreat-oriented
  navigation, with mining/cargo/service only as external context; or
- mining/cargo/offload control, with threat response and defense external; or
- navigation, docking, offload, and resupply turnaround control, with mining
  and threat response external.

The selected system may observe a contextual inhibition from an excluded
scope, but it must not reconstruct the replicate-1 five-way action arbitration
or use the same operational phase/recovery topology as its organizing policy.
Explain why it is not an extension or renaming of the closest baseline and
identify at least two genuine differences among boundary, state ownership,
interaction topology, priority policy, and recovery policy.
