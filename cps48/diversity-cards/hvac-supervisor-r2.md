# Automotive HVAC domain — replicate 2 diversity card

The accepted replicate-1 systems cover windshield-defrost authorization,
energy/interface-aware thermal service selection, and visibility-priority
whole-cabin HVAC control. They already use defrost, heating/cooling, electrical
eligibility, energy restriction, climate priority, and fresh-request recovery
as principal policy material.

Replicate 2 must not synthesize another defrost supervisor, thermal-service or
energy allocator, visibility-priority whole-cabin controller, or a renamed or
expanded version of those systems. Select a different primary controlled
purpose grounded by the seed: cabin air-quality and humidity supervision, or a
user-facing HVAC command coordinator whose principal state and outcomes concern
preference acceptance rather than climate actuation. Defrost and thermal/energy
service may appear only as external context or downstream effects.

Explain why the selected system is not an extension or repartitioning of the
closest replicate-1 baseline and identify at least two genuine differences
among boundary, state ownership, interaction topology, priority policy, and
recovery policy.
